# DHD and wormhole AssetBundle follow-up

## Objective

Extend the accepted Milky Way gate bundle with the DHD and the event horizon/Kawoosh. Preserve the current gameplay state machine, interaction, collision and OBJ renderer as fallback. Evaluate each addition on Development before changing the shipped asset-mode default or releasing it.

## Source and geometry baseline

- The DHD source is the user-supplied `Stargate DHD.zip` / `DHD.blend` documented in `src/assets/models/dhd/supplied-dhd-SOURCE-NOTICE.txt`. The original `.blend` contains 55,174 mesh polygons, or 110,796 triangles after triangulation, excluding its four-triangle `Light planes` helper. The authored Mirror on `DHD base` doubles that base's 11,340 triangles, giving 122,136 triangles before the later reductions and with subdivision disabled. These counts come from the Blender 2.71 file's Mesh and MPoly blocks, not from an evaluated subdivision render.

The [film effects download page](https://www.gian-cursio.net/2021/12/stargate-2020-effects-film-version/) labels Gian-Cursio's LightWave effect package CC0 and says it was dedicated to the public domain. Keep the original LightWave scene outside the repository and distribute only the derived runtime effect. The franchise's separate third-party rights are not addressed by that model license.
- The active supplied DHD OBJ parts contain 43,863 triangles total: body 10,401, accents 7,971, keys 14,286, symbols 10,917 and activation dome 288. The seven animated key and symbol groups partition those totals; they must not be counted a second time. Their exact counts and hashes are in `src/assets/models/dhd/supplied-dhd-build-report.json`.
- Player refinement: use the original DHD control mesh without reduction as LOD0 and the accepted 43,863-triangle OBJ as LOD1. The source's additional subdivision remains disabled. `build-original-dhd-lod0.py` applies the authored base Mirror, preserves all ten visual objects and partitions the seven button/symbol circuits. It yields 122,129 usable triangles after discarding seven rounded degenerates from the 122,136 source triangles. Both LODs keep corresponding circuit groups.
- The current horizon is procedurally animated (`WormholeAssets`, `GateWormholeVisual`, `WormholeLoop`, `WormholeTransition`). It uses two 70-frame atlas textures, a surge texture, a dissolve atlas, 140 loop meshes, 12 closing meshes and OBJ surge/rear-vortex parts. `scripts/assets/build-wormhole.py` and `scripts/assets/build-puddle-*.py` are the provenance.
- Inspection of `S:/Stargate/Stargate-MilkyWay_FBX_Animation_Templates` found gate ring/Chevron meshes and animation stacks, but no named horizon, puddle or Kawoosh mesh. `S:/Stargate/SG_Movie_Kawoosh_Demo` has a separate LightWave movie-gate Kawoosh patch (133 vertices, 132 patches, 252 base triangles) with `Kawoosh_Start` and `Kawoosh_End` morph maps. Both morph targets flatten the 4.194 m protrusion to a disk. The LightWave scene animates those targets around its opening and withdrawal. This is a useful shape reference, but its scene and gate scale differ from the current Milky Way effect.
- `build-lightwave-kawoosh.py` produced a local comparison mesh with 4,032 smoothed triangles, normalized depth 1 and radius 2.31 m; `WormholePose` can supply the current 0..3.78 m depth. Keep it separate from the accepted surge until a Development client comparison. The movie demo's source terms are not documented by the Milky Way FBX readme, so review its distribution terms before packaging it publicly.

## Constraints and affected systems

- Owner: `rw-plugin-oz-stargate` only. Unity 6000.3.22f1 builds the Windows, Linux and macOS bundle segments. Keep the original `.blend` and source FBXs outside the plugin; preserve the DHD source/license notices.
- The gate's bundled HDRP materials rendered magenta in Rising World. Include authored materials in the prefabs, but retain native `MaterialAsset` overrides until a client test proves the bundled DHD/effect materials render correctly. Do not regress the accepted gate materials.
- Bundle lights and collision must be prefab components. Runtime may toggle their named nodes to follow relay state; it must not create per-DHD lights in the bundle path. The OBJ fallback retains its existing lights/collision. Build the DHD as a separate `stargatedhd.bundle` so the accepted gate-only bundle hash is retained.
- DHD model placement currently stores `Model` and casts to `AnimatedDhdModel`; a renderer interface or equivalent focused adapter is needed to support the prefab without changing persistence or dial authority.
- The horizon's mesh/atlas frames are synchronized to `WormholePose`. A bundle implementation must retain opening, loop, close/dissolve, front/rear view, surge, rear vortex and horizon lighting. Avoid adding per-frame network mutations beyond the current implementation without measuring them.
- Use the project's [2026 reference notes](../reference/stargate-reference-information-2026.md) as visual checks: compare the opening's Kawoosh silhouette and radial highlights in front and profile views; inspect the water loop from oblique and rear angles. The film effect is only a shape and motion reference for this SG-1 gate, not a drop-in shader or asset. The notes do not alter relay timing or opaque gate IDs.

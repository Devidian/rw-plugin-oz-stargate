Accessory Pack 1 platform source: S:/Stargate/Stargate_2020_Accessory_Pack_1
Creator: David Gian-Cursio
Download and model description: https://www.gian-cursio.net/2023/11/stargate-2020-accessory-pack-1/
Source files remain outside this plugin. The runtime AssetBundle contains only
derived FBX LOD0 meshes for the curved and Grace models, a complete OBJ LOD0
for circular, reduced OBJ LOD1 meshes, collision meshes, prefabs and
the reused Stargate stone texture. Rebuild inputs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
scripts/assets/build-accessory-platforms.py and its generated manifest.json.
The author offers the pack for download and describes custom texturing and
optional lights in the four Grace bollards. The page does not state a specific
license for this pack; retain provenance and review distribution terms before
a public release. No original FBX, OBJ or LightWave source file is packaged.

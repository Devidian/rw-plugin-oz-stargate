Stargate (Milky Way Version) OBJ Conversion Kit
By David Gian-Cursio
March 28, 2021

Description:

These are mesh files from my model of the Milky Way version of the Stargate setpiece from "Stargate SG-1" in the .obj file format.

How to Use:

SG_MW_Main is the main body of the object. 

SG_MW_InnerRing_Earth_Giza is the rotating inner track found on the stargate discovered in Egypt, and used by the SGC most of the time, with the iconic pyramid-with-a-sun point of origin symbol. 

SG_MW_Chevron is the lower portion of the front-facing animated chevron, and should be duplicated to make a total of nine instances, rotated at increments of 40¡. When animating the chevron locking, they translate down 10.4 cm, and inward 2.3 cm.

SG_MW_ChevronBlock is the upper portions of the chevron. There should be a total of nine instances, at 40¡ increments. When animating the chevron locking, they translate up 3.5 cm.

The model has a three-layer texture setup. The base is a medium-gray stone texture, above that are larger, soft patterns of dark gray, and at the top are smaller splotches of light gray. The front faces of the symbols on the inner ring have an additional bump-map, giving a texture similar to grip-tape or coarse sandpaper. The lighted sections are a transparent dark red, while the lights themselves are more yellow. These surfaces are achieved procedurally in the Lightwave version of the model. Texture reference images of each PBR channel for the main surface, as well as renders of the original model, are available at https://www.gian-cursio.net/2021/02/stargate-2020-SG1-showcase/

The model is slightly out-of-scale, and should be scaled to 90% of its size (assuming it imports 1:1 to your program), so the outer diameter of the main ring is exactly 20 feet, or 6.096 meters. Positioning instructions assume the modeled size, not the rescaled size.
